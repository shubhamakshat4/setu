# Before launch: phone test checklist

Open the **live** link by tapping it inside a WhatsApp chat, the way your audience will. Try one Android and one iPhone if you can.

## First impression
- [ ] The WhatsApp link preview shows the building photo and "Setu — Be the bridge between classroom and career"
- [ ] A blurred version of the photo appears instantly, then the sharp photo fades in. There's no blank flash.
- [ ] The moon and dome are visible at the top; the building slowly "breathes" (zooms)
- [ ] "Setu" and the subhead rise in word by word; all white text is easy to read
- [ ] Nothing is cut off at the bottom by the browser bar; the scroll cue is visible
- [ ] The SSU logo is crisp, top-left
- [ ] The page never scrolls sideways

## Scrolling
- [ ] Sections fade up gently as you scroll; the gold line under each heading draws itself
- [ ] The bridge drawing draws itself when it comes into view
- [ ] Cards lift slightly when tapped
- [ ] The photo reappears as a divider band and behind "Become a mentor"
- [ ] The footer email opens your mail app; the privacy note is readable

## Sharing
- [ ] "Pass it on to someone perfect" opens a bottom panel
- [ ] **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the invitation message and link filled in
- [ ] **Copy link** shows "Copied! ✓", and pasting gives the page link
- [ ] **Share** (where shown) opens the phone's share menu

## The form
- [ ] "I'd love to be a mentor 🙏" opens the full-screen form over the blurred campus photo
- [ ] One question per screen; slides smoothly; progress bar moves; "Great start!", "Halfway there ✨", "Almost done! 🎉" appear
- [ ] Tapping **Next** on an empty name shows a friendly message
- [ ] Phone: `98765 4321` → "looks a digit short 🤔"; `+91 98765 43210` and `098765 43210` are accepted
- [ ] The keyboard shows numbers for the phone field and `@` for the email field
- [ ] Choosing "Under 3 yrs" shows "Early-career voices are valuable too!"
- [ ] Picking "Other" under domains shows a text box
- [ ] Opened via `?ref=bbsr-teachers`: "How did you hear about Setu?" is pre-filled
- [ ] The message box counts up to 500 and stops
- [ ] "Review my answers" stays disabled until consent is ticked
- [ ] Review screen lists every answer; **Edit** jumps to that question and "Back to review" returns
- [ ] **Autosave:** half-fill the form, close the tab, reopen the link, tap the button → your answers are still there
- [ ] The phone's **Back** button closes the form (doesn't leave the page)
- [ ] Every button is easy to tap with a thumb

## Submitting
- [ ] The button says "Building your bridge… 🌉" while sending
- [ ] Turn on flight mode and submit → "Something went wrong, but your answers are saved…" appears and answers remain. Turn flight mode off and submit again → success.
- [ ] Gold confetti falls slowly; "Welcome to the Setu family, [first name]! 🙏"
- [ ] There is **no** yellow "Demo mode" note (if there is, `SCRIPT_URL` isn't set)
- [ ] The badge shows the building, gold bridge, your first name and "I'm a Setu Mentor 🌉"
- [ ] **Download badge** saves the image; **Share** offers WhatsApp/Instagram with the image
- [ ] Share links on the thank-you screen end with `?ref=<your first name>`

## Google Sheet & email
- [ ] A new row appears in **Responses**, with the time in IST
- [ ] Phone shows as `+91XXXXXXXXXX`
- [ ] Submitting again with the same email updates the row and fills **Last Updated**
- [ ] The welcome email arrives (check Spam) and looks right on your phone
- [ ] Your admin notification arrives
- [ ] **Delete all test rows** before sharing the link

## Accessibility quick checks
- [ ] Phone setting "Reduce motion" (iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Motion; Android: Remove animations) → no zooming, parallax or confetti; content simply fades
- [ ] On a computer, you can complete the whole form with only the keyboard (Tab, Space, Enter); focus rings are visible
- [ ] With text size set to "Large" on the phone, nothing overlaps
